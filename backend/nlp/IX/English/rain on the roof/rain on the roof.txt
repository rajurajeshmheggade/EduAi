RAIN ROOF When sky covered dark clouds starts raining, have you ever listened patter soft rain roof ? What thoughts flashed through your mind you heard melody nature? Read poem find out what poet dreamed while listening rain. When humid shadows hover Over all starry spheres melancholy darkness Gently weeps rainy tears, What bliss press pillow cottage-chamber bed lie listening patter soft rain overhead! Every tinkle shingles Has echo heart; thousand dreamy fancies Into busy being start, thousand recollections Weave their air-threads into woof, I listen patter rain upon roof. Now memory comes my mother, she used years agone, regard darling dreamers Ere she left them till dawn: O! I feel her fond look me I list refrain Which played upon shingles patter rain.
