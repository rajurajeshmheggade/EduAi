WIND wind blows strongly causes lot destruction. How can we make friends it? Wind, come softly. Don’t break shutters windows. Don’t scatter papers. Don’t throw down books shelf. There, look what you did — you threw them all down. You tore pages books. You brought rain again. You’re very clever poking fun weaklings. Frail crumbling houses, crumbling doors, crumbling rafters, crumbling wood, crumbling bodies, crumbling lives, crumbling hearts — wind god winnows crushes them all. He won’t do what you tell him. So, come, let’s build strong homes, Let’s joint doors firmly. Practise firm body. Make heart steadfast. Do this, wind will friends us. wind blows out weak fires. He makes strong fires roar flourish. His friendship good. We praise him every day. SUBRAMANIA BHARATI [translated Tamil A.K. Ramanujan]
