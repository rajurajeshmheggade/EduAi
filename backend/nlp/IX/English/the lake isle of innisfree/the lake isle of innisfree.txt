LAKE ISLE INNISFREE well known poem explores poet’s longing peace tranquillity Innisfree, place where he spent lot time boy. poem lyric. I will arise go now, go Innisfree, small cabin build there, clay wattles made: Nine bean-rows will I have there, hive honeybee, live alone bee-loud glade. I shall have some peace there, peace comes dropping slow Dropping veils morning where cricket sings; There midnight’s all glimmer, noon purple glow, evenings full linnet’s wings. I will arise go now, always night day I hear lake water lapping low sounds shore; While I stand roadway, or pavements grey, I hear deep heart’s core. WILLIAM BUTLER YEATS
